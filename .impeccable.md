## Design Context

### Users
Non-technical internal users who need to turn meeting audio or video recordings into transcripts, searchable notes, and summaries. The context is operational: local files, long-running processing, clear job status, and low-friction retrieval of past material.

### Brand Personality
Solid, precise, and private. The interface should feel like a carefully maintained internal tool, not a landing page or decorative experiment.

### Aesthetic Direction
Local web application with a clear industrial aesthetic: readable surfaces, compact hierarchy, restrained green and copper accents, and a professional tool layout. The light theme should be suitable for office use and long text review.

### Design Principles
- Make every long-running step visible: ingestion, conversion, transcription, persistence, and summarization.
- Keep advanced configuration available without making it intrusive.
- Use direct Italian UI copy without unnecessary technical jargon.
- Clearly separate current work, historical library, and AI settings.
- Prioritize reliability, error recovery, and accessibility over decorative effects.
